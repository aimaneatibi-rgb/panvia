More than a listing

Panvia as a sales channel for your project

What an estate agent sells you as "sales support" is simply in your account here.

Interested buyers before the sale starts

Put your project online before the first pile goes into the ground. Interested buyers sign up for free — they don't need a buyer account, so your list fills up from day one. When the sale starts, you email a warm audience instead of a cold database.

Your own project page

Your own name, your own renders, your phasing, your types and your downloads. Buyers see your project, not a row in a list. And you link straight to it from your own Instagram and Facebook ads.

Visible in the right listings

A holiday park sits among the holiday homes, a complex among the homes, business units among the commercial property. Buyers searching there come across your project naturally — with the filters that belong to that segment.

Lead reporting

How many people looked, where they came from, which type attracts the most interest. Numbers you use to steer your own advertising budget — not a middleman's summary.

Direct contact

Serious buyers talk and bid directly with your sales team. No resold leads, no agency in between. Anyone who chats or bids has a paid buyer account — so you're not talking to time-wasters.

Widget on your own site

From Project M you put your project's live availability on your own website. Sell a unit and it's correct everywhere at once — you maintain one place.

Honest about where we stand

Who it's for

Who can list a project here?

Yes
  • Holiday parks selling their own recreation homes
  • Developers selling their own complex or project
  • Investors and property managers selling from their own portfolio
  • Housing associations with a sales mandate from the owner
No
  • Estate agents and intermediaries selling on behalf of a client
  • Offering or promoting services to others — violations carry the € 10,000 fine

Promoting your own project is of course fine: your brand, your renders, your story. The ban is about offering services to other users. Read the rules.
