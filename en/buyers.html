What you get

One subscription, direct contact

Talk to the owner

Ask your questions, plan a viewing and negotiate directly — with the person who can decide, not with an office that calls you back. Panvia doesn't read along.

Make an offer

Make an offer directly in the conversation. Not binding — you arrange the sale together at the civil-law notary — but seen by the person who's selling.

Contact on your terms — when it gets serious

The owner's name and phone number aren't published anywhere. The owner shares them personally, in the conversation, as soon as they see you mean it. So you talk directly, while the seller stays in control of their details.

Without a subscription you still see the full listings: photos, asking price, description and features. Only talking and bidding sit behind the subscription.

Seller details are protected. They only become visible when the owner shares them in the chat, only there, watermarked with your name. Forwarding or screenshotting is therefore traceable — so don't.
