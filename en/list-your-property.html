    How would you like to pay?

    The same 24-week listing, only the payment rhythm differs. The term starts as soon as your listing goes live. If you pay in instalments and sell before the end, we charge the remainder up to € 895 excl. VAT once — so you never pay more for selling quickly. If you stop without a sale, the payments stop and your listing goes offline. You choose your payment method — iDEAL, credit card or Bancontact — in Mollie's secure environment. No commission afterwards, not even if your property sells. Renewing is your decision.

    Ten units or more? For parks, complexes and developments you pay per project instead of per home — from € 4,150 per quarter. See Panvia Projects.

    What you get from us: 24 weeks of visibility, direct contact with buyers, checklists for the sale and the route to a civil-law notary. What we don't do: valuations, negotiating, brokering. That's your part.
