How we handle cookies

Panvia uses as few cookies as possible. Anything strictly necessary to make the site work — such as staying logged in — we always set. Everything else only after you give consent, and you can change that choice below at any time.

Want to change or withdraw your choice? One click is all it takes — the banner will appear again.

At the moment Panvia only sets strictly necessary storage: a session cookie once you log in, and local storage for your input on this device. No consent is required for that. As soon as we add tools that do require consent (such as statistics or ad measurement), the full cookie declaration will automatically appear here.
