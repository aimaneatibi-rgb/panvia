About Panvia

We give owners their ownership back.

The property market has one strange rule: estate agency firms have divided the visibility among themselves, so as an owner you first have to hire a firm just to be found at all. We believe selling shouldn't mean dependency. It's your property, your value and your decision.

What we stand for

Three convictions, no small print

Independence

You don't need anyone to be visible. No mandatory middleman, no gatekeeper, no asking permission to sell your own property. Anyone who does want guidance chooses it themselves — it's never a condition.

Ownership

The value of your property stays with you. No percentage of your proceeds for work you do yourself, no lead sold on to someone else. You set the price, you have the conversation, you sign at the notary.

Clarity

Every amount is on the site up front and doesn't change afterwards. Every view count is real. And if something isn't right for you, we say so — honesty here isn't a campaign, it's how we work.

Our mission

Enabling every owner to sell their property themselves — findable, safe and direct, for one fixed price. From terraced house to warehouse, from holiday home to portfolio, in the Netherlands and beyond.

Our vision

A property market where ownership is the norm: selling is something you do, not something you outsource. Owners and buyers find each other directly, and nobody depends on a middleman to be visible anymore.

Why this works

Ownership matters to buyers too

If you buy here, you talk to the person who can decide — not to an office that calls you back. You hear the real story, see the real numbers and make your offer directly. That's not a detail; that's how buying should feel.

What we do — and deliberately don't

We do
  • Make your property findable, for 24 weeks
  • Direct contact and offers between you and the buyer
  • Checklists and honest information for the whole sale
  • Point you to a civil-law notary near you
  • Verify ownership and keep estate agents out
Deliberately not
  • We don't do valuations — you set your price, if need be with an independent valuer who works for you
  • We don't negotiate and we don't join viewings
  • We don't broker — not even mortgages or financing; for that you go to your bank or an independent adviser
  • We never sell your data — to anyone, ever

Why so strict? The moment we broker "just a little", we're a middleman — and the promise is gone. Panvia provides information, not advice.

It's your property. So you sell it.
