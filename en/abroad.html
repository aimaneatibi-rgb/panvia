Buying across the border — what you should know. Every country has its own purchase process. In Spain you buy through a notario and usually a gestor, in France through a notaire, in Portugal through an advogado, in Italy through a notaio. Expect local taxes and transfer costs on top of the asking price.

Panvia makes the property findable and connects you with the owner. We don't do valuations, don't broker and don't give legal or tax advice — for that, engage a local professional. We provide information, not advice.
